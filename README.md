# Any AI system that answers from its own head will get something wrong in front of your customer.

My whole job is making the answer come from your data.

Business automation, lead systems, document assistants, chat and voice agents for businesses in Egypt, Iraq and the Gulf. Works in Egyptian, Iraqi, Gulf and Kurdish Sorani dialects.

## 🚀 What gets built

- 🎯 **Lead outreach and follow-up systems**
- 📚 **Assistants that answer from company documents**
- 🤖 **Chat and voice agents** for booking, orders and product search
- 🔗 **Workflows** connecting CRMs, stores and messaging tools
